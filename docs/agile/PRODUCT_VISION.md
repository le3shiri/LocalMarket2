# Product Vision

**For** the owner, staff and customers of LocalMarket, a grocery business in Barcelona,
**who** today depend on Excel files that are easy to break and do not reflect real stock,
**LocalMarket** is a web platform
**that** keeps one reliable source of truth for products, stock per location, online orders and payments,
**unlike** shared spreadsheets,
**our product** records who changed what, prevents overselling, and lets customers order online for
home delivery or store pickup.

## Goals
1. Reliable stock: every change attributed and traceable.
2. Online sales without overselling.
3. Simple daily work for warehouse and store staff.
4. A friendly Spanish shopping experience for customers.

## Success measures (proposed)
- 0 orders accepted without stock in tests and demo.
- 100 % of stock changes have user + reason.
- A new customer can place an order in under 3 minutes.
